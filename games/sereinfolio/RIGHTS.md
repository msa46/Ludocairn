## Provenance

Sereinfolio is an original non-divinatory reflection and storytelling
activity. It uses general, unprotected procedures such as drawing, observing,
prompting, sharing, passing, and taking rounds, together with canonical tarot
card names. Every prompt and instruction was independently written. No deck
imagery, guidebook meaning, published spread, rulebook wording, branding,
software data, or other third-party expressive content was copied or adapted.

## Name clearance

Preliminary searches were performed on 2026-08-21 before this title was added.

- The planned title `Lantern Arc` was rejected because the preliminary screen
  found an active indie-games identity using `Lantern Arcade`.
- The first replacement, `Vellum Constellation`, was rejected after the
  confusing-similarity screen found the active commercial game
  [Vellum](https://store.steampowered.com/app/917950/Vellum/), the active
  `Vellum Games` and `Vellum Interactive` identities, and tabletop software
  using `Vellum` prominently.
- A second candidate, `Quillora Mosaic`, was rejected after the screen found an
  active [Quillora iOS app](https://quillora.app/) and an existing `QUILLORA`
  software-service trademark record (USPTO serial 98325680).
- `Sereinfolio` was then selected as a coined title combining *serein*, an
  evening calm, with *folio*.
- The [USPTO Trademark Search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/) returned no
  result for exact combined-mark query `CM:"sereinfolio"` or expanded searches
  for `sereinfol`, `serenfol`, and `sereinfolio` spelling roots.
- [EUIPO eSearch plus](https://euipo.europa.eu/eSearch/) returned zero trade
  marks for both `"Sereinfolio"` and `Sereinfolio`.
- [TMview](https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/) returned no rows for either exact
  (`Is`) or fuzzy searches for `Sereinfolio`. Participating offices shown
  during the search included BX (Benelux), EM (EUIPO), US, and WO (WIPO).
- Exact-name searches of the general web, BoardGameGeek, GitHub, npm, Apple
  App Store, Google Play, Steam, and common game results found no use of
  `Sereinfolio`.
- The direct interactive BOIP result screen was not separately relied on;
  TMview included BX data but does not replace a search and opinion by a
  qualified professional.
