## Provenance

Veilquorum is an original work using general, unprotected game procedures such
as hidden roles, alternating phases, private selection, discussion, voting,
and elimination. It also uses common facts about a standard 52-card deck. No
published rulebook wording, branded characters, setting, artwork, logo,
software data, or third-party card design was copied or adapted.

## Name clearance

Preliminary searches were performed on 2026-08-21 before this title was added.

- The earlier candidate `Signal Cairn` was rejected after the fused form
  `SignalCairn` appeared in a commercial naming catalog and active `Cairn` uses
  were found in software and games.
- The [USPTO Trademark Search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/) returned no
  live or dead wordmark result for `Veilquorum`.
- [EUIPO eSearch plus](https://euipo.europa.eu/eSearch/) returned zero trade
  marks for `Veilquorum`.
- [TMview](https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/) returned no rows for either an exact
  (`Is`) or fuzzy search. Its participating offices included BX (Benelux), EM
  (EUIPO), US, and WO (WIPO) during the search.
- Exact-name searches of the public web, BoardGameGeek, GitHub, npm, and common
  app-store results returned no use of `Veilquorum`.
- The direct interactive BOIP result screen was unavailable during the check;
  the TMview search included BX data but does not replace an official legal
  clearance opinion.

This is a preliminary conflict screen, not legal advice or a guarantee of
registrability or freedom to use. Similar unregistered uses may exist, and
database coverage and market use can change.
